-WackyRecipes-

WackyRecipes (or Wacky Recipe Generator) is a website that gives you strange meals/food combinations to make!

If you want a recipe randomly given to you, press "GENERATE" at the top. if you want a complete list of all recipes available, press "Collection"

NO AI IS USED FOR THIS PROJECT

100% human-made project. All recipes are human made and are found by scouring websites and obscure reddit threads. (mostly reddit threads)

Where do I start?

Start by clicking "GENERATE" at the top or press the button below!
